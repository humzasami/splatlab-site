How to play

  1. Pick a scene and a weapon on the home screen, then tap PLAY.
  2. Watch and listen. Flies buzz while they fly; when the buzzing stops, one has landed and the weapon's edge glows red.
  3. On a foldable iPhone: unfold the phone so one half shows the scene and the other the weapon, then fold it shut to slam the weapon down.
  4. On any other iPhone: swipe the weapon panel across the scene to slam it down.

You have three lives, and a slam that hits nothing costs one. Squash two flies in a single slam to win a life back.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need a foldable iPhone?

No. SplatLab works on every supported iPhone. Folding is the most fun way to play, but swiping works everywhere.

My foldable iPhone keeps asking me to unfold it

Start on the cover screen, tap PLAY and unfold the phone. To play on the cover screen only, tap Play on this screen instead.

Can I put the weapon on the other side?

Yes. Go to Settings › Weapon side when unfolded, or use the button in the pause menu during a game.

Can I turn off swiping and only use the fold?

Yes. On a foldable iPhone, turn off Settings › Allow swipe-to-slam.

There's no sound

Make sure Sound is on in the app's Settings and that your volume is turned up. If you'd like the game to stay quiet when your phone is on silent, turn off Play sound in silent mode.

How do I turn off vibrations?

Turn off Settings › Haptics.

How do I reset my scores?

Go to Settings › Reset progress. This erases your scores and stats and cannot be undone.

Will I lose my progress if I change phones?

Progress is stored on your iPhone and moves to a new phone when you restore from an iCloud or computer backup. Deleting the app erases it.

Does the game have ads or in-app purchases?

No. There are no ads, no in-app purchases and no accounts.

Does the app collect my data?

No. SplatLab collects no personal data and works entirely offline. See our Privacy Policy.

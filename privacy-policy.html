Privacy Policy

SplatLab: Fold & Swat · Effective 13 November 2026

In short: SplatLab does not collect, store on any server, sell or share any personal data. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. Your game progress stays on your iPhone.

This policy explains how the SplatLab: Fold & Swat app for iPhone ("SplatLab", "the app") handles information. The app is developed and published by Humza Sami Chughtai ("we", "us").

1. Information we collect

We do not collect any personal information. The app does not ask for your name, email address, phone number, location, contacts, photos or any other personal details, and it has no sign-in or account system.

The app does not connect to the internet and does not send any information to us or to anyone else.

2. Information stored on your device

To save your progress and preferences, the app keeps a small amount of game data on your iPhone using Apple's standard on-device storage:

  • best scores, total flies squashed, games played, best combo and daily streak;
  • your selected scene and weapon;
  • settings such as sound, haptics, silent-mode sound, swipe-to-slam and weapon side.

This data never leaves your device and is not accessible to us. It may be included in your own device backups (for example, iCloud Backup), which are handled by Apple under Apple's privacy policy.

3. Device sensors

On foldable iPhones, the app reads the hinge position to detect when you fold the phone to swat a fly. On other iPhones it may read the motion sensors for the same purpose. These readings are processed instantly on your device, are never stored and are never shared.

4. Third parties

The app contains no third-party advertising, analytics, tracking or social media software development kits (SDKs), and we do not share or sell data with any third party.

If you have chosen to share analytics with app developers in your iPhone's settings (Settings › Privacy & Security › Analytics & Improvements), Apple may provide us with anonymous, aggregated crash reports and usage statistics. These do not identify you and are governed by Apple's Privacy Policy. You can turn this off at any time in those settings.

5. Tracking

The app does not track you across apps or websites owned by other companies and does not use the advertising identifier.

6. Data retention and deletion

Because all game data is stored only on your device, you are in full control of it:

  • to erase your progress, open the app and go to Settings › Reset progress;
  • to remove all app data, delete the app from your iPhone.

We hold no copy of your data, so there is nothing for us to delete on our side.

7. Children's privacy

The app is suitable for all ages. It does not collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your country).

8. Your rights

Depending on where you live, laws such as the EU and UK General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) or the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA) may give you rights to access, correct or delete personal data. Since we do not collect or hold any personal data about you, there is no data for us to provide, correct or delete. If you have any questions about your rights, please contact us.

9. Security

Your game data is protected by your iPhone's built-in security, including device encryption and your passcode, Face ID or Touch ID.

10. Changes to this policy

If we change how the app handles information, we will update this page and the effective date above before the change takes effect. If a future version ever needed to collect data, we would update this policy and the App Store privacy details first.

11. Contact us

If you have any questions about this policy or the app, please email humzasamichg@gmail.com.
